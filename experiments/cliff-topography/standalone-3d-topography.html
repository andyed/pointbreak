Geological Mechanics: Cliff to Ramp

The Pleasure Point marine terrace exhibits a classic wave-cut platform morphology:

  • Upper Terrace (+10 m to +20 m): Relict Pleistocene marine terrace carrying East Cliff Drive.
  • Sea Cliff Face: Near-vertical cliff formed by wave undercutting of the Purisima Formation siltstone.
  • Wave-Cut Reef Ramp (MSL to −12 m): Modern submerged shore platform sloping gently seaward at a 1:58 ramp ($1.0^\circ$ slope), causing waves to shoal and break.
