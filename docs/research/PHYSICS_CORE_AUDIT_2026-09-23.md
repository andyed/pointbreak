# Physics-core audit: the model's numbers beside the textbook relations (2026-09-23)

**Finding.** The physics core is internally sound and its residuals against the
standard relations are invisible: the shipped shoaling coefficient matches exact
linear theory to 0.3 %, the 0.7/2.6 clamp and the `growSyn` stand-in are inert at
every shipped line, and the breaker sits inside the textbook envelope (Hb 6–12 %
under Komar & Gaughan; γ = 0.78 at the low end of Weggel's 0.82–1.07 on the
local slopes, which puts the drawn line 2–19 m seaward of Weggel's, inside the
bed's own 22–70 m crossing uncertainty). The residuals a surfer would see are
all authorship against the bed, not physics against physics: every preset's
authored Iribarren is 1.4–3.1× the ξ the bed at its own line supports (the bed
classifies all seven card states as spilling; five presets are authored
plunging, and the authored value is what draws the lip), the physical front
face is 10–15° against Carini's 22°/30° with the phase map's entire remaining
budget worth about 1°, and the set-peak setup is 0.3·H0 = 1.76× Guza &
Thornton's field regression (though inside the Longuet-Higgins/Bowen theory
band, and ×3.2 on screen). The one new result is on the peel angle: the
authored α is met at one depth only — the bake's own derived α clears Walker's
30° floor inside a spot-specific H0 window (Sewers 1.65–2.42 m, The Hook
1.20–1.65 m, Sharks 0.95–1.05 m, Jack's 0.88 to at least 1.54 m, First Peak
1.44 to at least 2.52 m; Second Peak and Privates never), because the line
bearing slides from the wedge's 34–36° toward 7–10° as the line walks seaward
while the crest bearing stays within 2°, so size takes the line off the fitted
reef's oblique band in *both* directions, and GAME_PROJECTION C3(1)'s "run the
spots nearer their ceiling — confirmed free" is not free at three of seven.

Basis for everything below unless a row says otherwise: the shipped NCEI 2012
interpolant (`ncei13`), tide 0, each preset's card T, H0 at 0.7×, 1.0× and 1.3×
the card, commit `a863e58`, measured through the shipped bake (`bed.js
bakeBreakLine` read back on the 2 m stage grid with `main.js stageAlpha`'s own
reductions: limiter-pinned and section-gap stations excluded). Every rung passed
`measure_size_headroom.mjs`'s gates G1–G3 (the excess field is bed.js's own to
0.000 m, the replica line is the bake's own to 0.000 m, the depth read under the
line satisfies the shipped criterion). The derived α reproduces
`params.js PEEL_FLOOR.alphaCard` to 0.1° at all six spots that record one, so
the reduction is the bake's, not a twin (MEASUREMENT_LESSONS 4). Rungs marked †
are below the spot's measured peel floor (MODEL.md 4.6); their α is the
documented inshore-branch collapse, not a peel, and is kept so the table is
honest about its own domain (LESSONS 14b).

Instrument: `scripts/measure_physics_residuals.mjs` (headless, 0.6 s for all
21 rungs plus the 105-rung window sweep). GPU leg: `scripts/probe_wave_shape.mjs`
(MODEL_GLSL verbatim, provenance flags `modelInVert` and `sliceIsSubstring`

## What each residual means for the picture

**R1 — breaker height and depth: invisible.** At a true crossing the model's
γb is 0.78 by construction (GAME_PROJECTION §0.2's identity), so the informative
comparison is against relations that do not assume it. Komar & Gaughan predict a
breaker 6–12 % taller at every rung; this is the coefficient, not the bed:
under linear shoaling to the break point their 0.56 corresponds to γb ≈ 1.43,
and with γ = 0.78 the same derivation gives 0.499, i.e. −11 %, which the
finite-kh Ks (+1–2 % over the shallow asymptote at kh 0.2–0.3) and the shelter
factor pull back to the −6 to −12 % measured. Weggel on the local slopes
(1.0–1.9° at the card lines, 0.4° at Privates) gives γb 0.82–1.00 at the card
(up to 1.07 at ×1.3), so the model breaks in 2–17 % deeper water than Weggel at
six spots and at Weggel's depth at Privates. Through the local slope that is a line 2–19 m seaward of Weggel's
(Sewers +19 m, First Peak +14, The Hook +10, Jack's +7, Second Peak +6, Sharks
+2, Privates −2). The bed's own 0.31–0.93 m elevation residual displaces the
same crossing 22–70 m (`bed.js`, onset-merge note), so no camera could tell the
two apart on this interpolant. Battjes & Stive's 0.53–0.60 is a random-wave Hrms
index and is reported only so nobody reads it as a target. Hb/H0 = 1.37–1.78 is
Ks at the line, as §0.2 already established.

**R2 — shoaling: invisible, and the claim is slightly misnamed.** The Guo-k
coefficient is within −0.2 to −0.3 % of the exact linear value at every line
(the Guo error at kh 0.18–0.30). Neither clamp is reached at any shipped line
(Ks 1.32–1.69) and `growSyn` cannot be reached: `u_depthMix = 1` on all seven
presets since Privates was mapped. MODEL.md 1.1 and 2.2 call this "Green's law";
the implementation is the full linear shoaling coefficient, of which Green's
h^(−1/4) is the shallow asymptote, and at these lines the two differ by 1–2 %.
A naming note for the coordinator, not a defect.

**R3 — breaker type: visible, and it is authorship.** The bed at the line
classifies every card state as spilling (ξ0 0.11–0.44); the authored ξ says
plunging at Sewers, First Peak, Second Peak, Jack's and The Hook. The authored
value is what reaches the pixels: `smoothstep(0.45, 1.25, u_xi)` sets `plunge`
in the lifecycle, the landing frame, the impact source, the classic descent and
the splash, and `u_xi` also sets the crest exponent `q` and the lip-foam gain,
while `iribarrenMeasured` reaches nothing (GAME_PROJECTION §0.2 already said
so; this table puts the size of the gap on it). The bed's shortfall is
moderate, not absurd: Sewers needs tan β ≥ 0.040 (2.3°) at its line for ξ0 to
reach 0.5, against 0.030 measured; First Peak crosses into plunging at ×1.3 on
its own. Local ξ0 rises with H0 at five of seven spots (the line migrates onto
steeper wedge), which is §0.2's finding again; `iribarrenMeasured` on the
plane-fit slope falls as H0^(−½) and says the opposite, so the HUD readout and
the bed disagree in sign about what size does to breaker type.

**R4 — peel angle and speed: visible, and the new finding.** At the card the
bake's along-line peel speed is 6.3–10.2 m/s at six spots (the 4.8–10 m/s band
VALIDATION_PLAN cites holds) and 14.0 m/s at Privates, whose card α is 15.7°.
cg-based zippers are within 3 % of c-based ones (n = 0.97–0.98 at kh 0.2–0.3),
so the choice of speed law is not a lever here. Where each preset lands on
Hutt et al.'s skill-limit table (the minimum α a rating can negotiate): the
authored bank asks for ratings 5–7 (α 31–50°), which is the amateur band and
consistent with Mead et al. (1997)'s "low peel angles produce world-class
breaks" only at Sewers and Privates; the derived α at the card puts Sewers,
Jack's, The Hook and Sharks at rating 7 (top amateur), First Peak at rating 3
(60.7°, learner-grade, the mellowest wave in the bank by the bake's own
reading, +10.7° over its 50° target), and Second Peak (25.8°) and Privates
(15.7°) below the rating-8 limit of 27°, i.e. faster than any rated surfer's
minimum and below Walker's 30° floor: closeouts by the table, which is what
MODEL.md 4.6 already says of Privates and what the Second Peak card has been
quietly doing (its 41° target is never met at any H0 in 0.7–1.4×; its maximum
is 27.8° at 1.43 m).

The window (R4b) is the part nobody had read. The standing
`qa/break-field/summary.json` sweep (2026-09-02, a different model version)
has the same decline in it — Sewers 38.9° at 2.0 m to 10.2° at 3.0 m, The Hook
40.3° at 1.3 m to 14.6° at 2.4 m, Sharks 30.5° at 1.0 m to 7.7° at 2.0 m — and
today's bake reproduces it at the shared rungs (Sewers 2.2 m: 36.34° then,
36.3° now). Two instruments, two dates, one trend, and the on-reef fraction
rules out the obvious mechanism: at Sewers the line's on-reef share *rises*
0.70 → 0.85 while α falls 38° → 18°. The bearings say what is happening. The
refracted crest bearing is flat across the whole ladder (Sewers −2.2° to
−4.2°, Second Peak −5.0° to −6.7°, The Hook −8.4° to −9.6°), so refraction is
essentially finished by hb and contributes nothing to the change; the *line*
bearing is what moves (Sewers 35.8° → 7.7°, The Hook 33.2° → 10.0°, Second
Peak 25.8° → 7.3°). The fitted wedge is oblique over a limited depth band
around the depth it was fitted at (`breakDepthFor(H0, T)` at the card), and
seaward of that band the composite contours turn back toward the DEM's
shore-parallel trend, so a line that breaks deeper follows a straighter
contour and α → 0. That is the same mechanism as the low-H0 collapse of 4.6,
arriving from the other side: the reef fit is a point fit in depth, and the
authored α is a declaration about one hb. In hb terms the ≥ 30° band is
Sewers 3.2–4.4 m, The Hook 2.3–3.0 m, Sharks 1.87–2.03 m (0.16 m of water),
Jack's 1.77 m to past 2.79 m, First Peak 2.75 m to past 4.33 m. A surfer sees
this directly: at Sewers 2.6 m the wave sections and closes out where 2.2 m
peeled. GAME_PROJECTION §0.2's "gap fraction falls 0.22 → 0.15, the peel gets
better" was true and vacuous (LESSONS 8c): the gap fraction summarises limiter
pins, α summarises the peel, and they moved in opposite directions.

**R5 — face angle: visible, and structural, with the stated reason wrong.**
The closed form on the model's own shape function gives a physical steepest
front face of 11.1–15.3° at the card lines at set peak; the GPU probe on the
raw drawn field reads 9.9–14.7° (medians over three sims and three stations;
the arbitrary set phase of a frozen sim clock is why it sits under the
set-peak closed form). Against Carini's 22° the deficit is 7–11°, ratio
0.45–0.70. The bracket shows why size cannot buy it and neither can the clamp:
the symmetric carrier (s = 0) is 7.5–10.6°, the shipped s = 0.8 adds 3.6–4.7°,
and the monotonicity limit s = 1 adds 0.7–0.9° more. The binding quantity is
the carrier steepness Hb/L = 0.025–0.034: a 12–15 s swell breaking in 1.5–4 m
of water is a long, low wave, and an even monotone phase map θ − s(1 − cos θ)
can compress its front face by at most a factor 1 + s ≤ 2 (measured 1.48–1.51
at the point of steepest slope). Reaching 22° needs a front slope of 0.40
against the shipped 0.20–0.27, i.e. 1.5–2.0× more than the map can give at
any s. One correction for the coordinator: MODEL.md 2.2 and the `ocean()`
comment say "s = 1 is a vertical face and s > 1 makes the map non-monotonic —
the height field would go multivalued". At s = 1 the map's derivative
1 − s·sin θ vanishes at θ = +π/2, which is the *back* face, and flattens it;
`h(x) = f(θ′(θ(x)))` is a function of x for every s and cannot be multivalued.
A non-monotone map retraces the shape function and draws a spurious hump on
the back. The clamp is right; its justification is not, and the front-face
gain from lifting it to 1.0 is under a degree. The GPU probe's *displaced*
curve reads 12.8–54° because it includes the `choppyPos` fold; Sewers is the
only spot where the fold is the face (49–60 folded bins per sim against 0–10
elsewhere), which is the lip anatomy, not the carrier, and out of this audit's
scope.

**R6 — setup: invisible physically, visible by design.** 0.3·H0 is 0.18–0.21·Hb
at the card, inside the 0.15–0.24·Hb band the theory spans for γ 0.6–0.78, and
0.76–0.89× the Longuet-Higgins/Bowen shoreline value K·hb with γ = 0.78
(0.71–0.82× at the small rungs, up to 0.93× at ×1.3). It is 1.76× Guza & Thornton's 0.17·Hs, but
that regression is on a random-wave significant height and the model's H0 is
a monochromatic carrier at set peak, so the two are not the same denominator.
The number a viewer meets is ×VIS = 0.96·H0 (0.67–2.11 m of shoreline breathe
at the card), which is the declared 2026-08-11 authorship (raised from 0.2 to
survive drone distance) and belongs in the VIS column, not the physics one.

## What would falsify this

* **R1/R3.** A surveyed sounding under any break (USGS OFR 2007-1270's
  SWATHplus, VALIDATION_PLAN "Ground truth status") whose slope at the line is
  ≥ 1.4× the interpolant's would move local ξ0 to plunging at Sewers and lift
  Weggel's γb; the authorship gap would then be the interpolant's, not the
  bank's. A measured breaking position on one day that lands outside the 2–19 m
  band would discriminate γ; inside it cannot.
* **R4b.** Run the app at Sewers with `#h0=2.6` through `3.0` and read the
  HUD's derived α: values ≥ 30° at 2.8 m would falsify the window. Rebuilding
  on the CUDEM interpolant (`--bed=cudem19`) and finding the line bearing does
  not turn shore-parallel seaward of the wedge would relocate it to the bed
  rather than the fit. The instrument's window is bounded by its own ladder
  (0.7–1.4×; open ends are marked, LESSONS 14b).
* **R5.** A GPU probe at the set anchor (env = 1) reading a raw front face
  > 17° physical at any card line would falsify the closed-form ceiling.
* **R2.** Nothing cheap: the residual is the published Guo error.
