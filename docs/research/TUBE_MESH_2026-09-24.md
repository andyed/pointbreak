# The swept breaker ribbon (`#tube=1`) — 2026-09-24

Track B of the six-agent fan-out. Base `c1e78ab`. Opt-in, not promoted, no
defaults changed: the default build is byte-identical to main at three cameras
(below).

## Finding

The overturn now exists as explicit geometry: a strip swept along the break
line whose cross-section is a shared breaker profile, C0 on the drawn water at
a back seam and at the shared landing, with a measured cavity of 0.62–0.67
crest heights under the jet at every probed station. Three things the design
assumed were falsified on the way and are recorded here because the next
track will meet them: capping the cusp parameter at 0.98 does **not** make the
height field single-valued; zeroing the bend stands the head block up to fill
1.48; and the ribbon reads as a **diagonal flap off the crest, not a barrel**,
at VIS 3.2 and at VIS 1 alike. The mechanism, seams and instruments are
verified. The picture is not accepted.

## Design

**Profile contract** — `shared/breaker-profile-glsl.js`, the interface Track A
replaces the body of:

```glsl
vec2  breakerProfile(float u, float age, float xi, float hC, float c);  // (s shoreward, y up), PHYSICAL m
float breakerProfileWeight(float age, float xi);                         // 0..1, zero at both lifecycle ends
```

`u = 0` is the back seam at `s = −0.5·h_C` on the back face, `u = 1` the
landing seam at `y = 0`. `age` is seconds since this station's crest crossed
the break line — `breakerCurlCycle`'s clock: 0 pitch, `CRASH_PEAK_S` 0.42
impact, released by 0.72. The shipped stub is a quarter-ellipse back arc
`(−0.5h_C, 0.6h_C) → (0, h_C)` arriving with a horizontal tangent, joined C1 to
the parabola `s = s_L·τ, y = h_C(1 − τ²)` with `s_L = clamp(c·√(2h_C/g), 0.9,
1.6)·h_C` (free fall at the phase speed, clamped to the band the shared landing
frame spans). Before impact the tip advances linearly in τ (free-fall
kinematics); after it the foot is pinned and the upper edge clears down the
curve to the foot by 0.72 s. Weight = Battjes plunge blend `smoothstep(0.45,
1.25, ξ)` × in-ramp (0–0.05 s) × out-ramp (0.60–0.72 s). Sharks (ξ 0.45) and
Privates (0.35) get exactly zero.

**Ribbon** — `web-three/js/tube.js`, `TUBE_VERT/FRAG` composed from
`SURFACE_PRELUDE + SURFACE_GLSL` (the profile is spliced into the prelude
under `#ifdef TUBE`). Mesh `PlaneGeometry(570, 1, 2400, 24)`: `position.x`
alongshore, `position.y + 0.5 = u`. Per vertex:

- **Clock and frame**: `breakerLandingFrameAt(x, t)` gives `age = .z +
  CRASH_PEAK_S`, the landing `z_L` and the ceiling. Chosen over recomputing
  choppyPos's `ageB` (the same `mod(ωt − rayPhase(x, breakLine(x)))/ω`)
  because one call also returns the landing, so the ribbon and the deposit,
  roller and spray cannot disagree about the foot.
- **Crest anchor**: the LIVE crest source `z_c = z_b + θ_W/k_z`
  (`CURTAIN_VERT`'s construction), `P_c = surfacePos(x, z_c)`. Not the impact
  pose — the seams are re-evaluated on the live surface, so the ribbon stays
  on the water that is drawn.
- **Sweep axis**: the `rayPhase` gradient at the crest (the classic block's
  `waveDir`), guarded shoreward.
- **Placement**: `P = P_c + dir·(s·σ) + ŷ·(y − h_C)·VIS`. `σ = D/s_L` where
  `D` is the drawn crest-to-drawn-landing distance along the axis, so the
  profile's physical landing maps onto the shared landing. The vertical is
  VIS-exaggerated like all water.
- **Seams**: `P_B = surfacePos` at the back-seam source, `P_L = surfacePos`
  at the landing source `(x, z_c) + dir·(z_L − z_c)/dir.y`. Blend weights
  `w_B = (1 − smoothstep(0, 0.15, u))·(1 − smoothstep(0, 0.25, clear))` and
  `w_L = smoothstep(0.85, 1, u)·smoothstep(0.75, 1, tip)`. **Contract:** C0 at
  the back seam while the sheet is attached (age ≤ 0.40, before the upper edge
  clears); C0 at the landing from impact on. A jet in flight has a free tip —
  that open edge is the mouth — so the landing seam is not asserted before
  0.42 s, and the back seam is released after impact so the cleared edge is
  not pulled back up the face (the defect LIP_DESCENT_EXPERIMENT removed).
- **Gate**: `weight × breakMask × farFade × (1 − land_crest)(1 − land_landing)`.
  Zero weight parks the vertex 2 m under the water with alpha 0 and skips all
  three `surfacePos` evaluations — which is what pays for 2400 columns (the
  curtain's 240 drew the ~4 m ribbon as two triangles).
- Normal by finite differences on the un-seamed profile (analytic in `u`,
  one extra `breakerLandingFrameAt` in `x`, guarded across the lifecycle
  wrap), oriented away from the cavity centre. NaN guards on P and N.
- Under `#mirror` the mesh hangs under `world` like the curtain.

**Shading**, modest and Track D's to improve: glassy exterior (sky fresnel
over a lit green-blue body, sun spec), green-black interior with a
fresnel-lit rim, thin-lip backlit green toward the sun, aerated streaks on the
simulation clock (`CURTAIN_FRAG`'s pattern) building toward impact.

**Build flag** — `#tube=1` is a build decision like `#roller` (`TUBE_BUILD`
in `main.js`): the grid, spray, curtain and `curlProbe` materials get
`defines.TUBE = 1`, the ribbon mesh is built, the curtain is hidden, `u_tube`
is the live gain (`__pointbreak.setTube`). The rider's surface query does
not carry the define (its `defines` literal is pinned by
`tests/breaker-anatomy.test.js`), so under the flag the rider rides the
un-carved grid at the lip only. Unknown hash keys are boot-only in
`url-params.js`, so `#tube` needed no registration there.

## What the grid gives up, and what it took to make it

The spec's handover was "cap S at 0.98 and zero the bend where the tube has
weight, so the height field stays single-valued". Measured, in order:

1. **S ≤ 0.98 is not single-valued.** At Sewers x=−52, impact, the transect's
   reverse travel in displaced z (the fold, in metres — a raw back-step count
   was tried first and measured chop jitter identically on both arms) was
   **10.8 m with the cap against 10.5 m with the shipped bend**. Sweeping the
   cap: 0.98 / 0.8 / 0.65 all read 10.85 (the pocket's effective S is already
   ~0.5–0.65 after its gates), 0.5 → 9.9, 0.4 → 7.1, **0.3 → 4.4 m**. The fold
   is the choppy convergence itself: the sharpened crest's curvature outruns
   the `a·k²` the S = 1 cusp criterion is derived from. Shipped: `u_tubeS =
   0.3` inside the handover window (JS-settable, `setTubeS`, for the sweep).
2. **Zeroing the bend stands the block up.** At the head station (x=−49,
   sim 48) the tube arm's crest stood at **11.33 m over a 7.63 m ceiling
   (fill 1.48), curl 0**, where the shipped bend had folded it to 8.13 m (fill
   1.07, curl 0.45). The earn floor was the only outlet over-fill had. That
   block was the white slab in the first captures; hiding the ribbon changed
   nothing, because the ribbon was inside it.
3. **A flat soft ceiling makes a mesa.** Replacing the bend with a pocket-gated
   tanh ceiling at fill 1.03 brought the crest to 8.02 m (fill 1.05) — and
   the probe's cavity fell to **0.03 h_C**: a 4 m plateau at the ceiling with
   the jet lying inside it (x=−84 failed the 0.3 h_C assertion).
4. **Shipped: a carved ceiling.** Ahead of the crest phase the allowed fill
   falls as `1.03 − 1.4·√(dz_C/h_C)`, floored at the bend line 0.35 —
   near-vertical at the lip, easing down the face; flat 1.03 behind the
   crest. The water above the carve is the jet, which the ribbon draws.
   Cavity back to 0.64–0.67 h_C at all three stations.

All of it is gated by `tubeGate = plunge × smoothstep(0, 0.2, age) × (1 −
smoothstep(0.9, 1.4, age))` — zero ahead of the head (age ≈ T), held through
the ribbon's life, released into the bore. The bend itself is zeroed at
plunging stations for every age (`bendOnset *= 1 − tubeChar`). The grid's lip
aeration, which keyed on the now-zero `curl`, keys instead on the profile
weight at the station's age, inside a `0.35 h_C` crest band (the first cut
keyed on `pocket` alone and painted a 25 m white plate).

The seam gaps are exactly zero because both ends ARE `surfacePos` evaluations
blended at weight 1; the tolerance in the script is 0.05 m. The profile's
landing sits on the 1.6 clamp (free fall at the ~5 m/s phase speed wants
~1.55). The drawn reach is 0.35–0.5 h_C through the pitch: the shipped
landing is 0.9 h_C ahead in source space and the convergence compresses it to
~3.5 m drawn — the ribbon is 2–3 times taller than wide.

**With the shared frame's classic-descent receiver** (`#tube=1&classic=1&descent=1`,
`qa/tube-2026-09-24/probe.receiver.json`): drawn reach **1.0–1.6 h_C**, cavity
0.62–0.64 h_C, and the grid's fold is **exactly 0 m at every age** (the
classic arm's smooth carrier-phase convergence plus the 0.3 cap). That is the
configuration in which the height field is genuinely single-valued under the
ribbon. It was not made the default here because `classic` also changes the
shoulder everywhere and is itself unaccepted.

**Default parity** (`scripts/capture_tube_ab.mjs`, `MAIN_URL` on a `git
archive c1e78ab` tree): a default boot on this branch against pristine main
is **byte-identical** as PNG at the Sewers close camera (sim 48), Sewers
`#cam=cliff` (sim 42) and Second Peak `#cam=lookout` day=big (sim 48). The
handover and the profile are under `#ifdef TUBE`, so the default compiles the
pristine shader text; the only default-path source change is the import.

**Suite**: `npm test` 234/234 before every commit. `check-shader-literals` now
lints the two new GLSL files.

## What still looks wrong

1. **A flap, not a tube.** Each station's profile is at a different age, so
   the ribbon is a ramp from the young crest to the old landing over a few
   metres of line. The picture of a barrel needs the jet to hang at mid-flight
   over several crest heights of line — i.e. a lifecycle several times longer
   than 0.72 s, or a peel several times faster, or a roof that stays after the
   foot lands. The shared clock forbids the first two; the third is a profile
   question for Track A (a post-impact roof that thins rather than clearing).
2. **Tall and narrow at VIS 3.2.** Drawn reach 0.35–0.5 h_C on the default
   landing. The receiver bundle fixes the width (1.0–1.6) and the fold (0 m)
   at once, and is the arm to judge.
3. **Residual fold, 1.3–4.5 m** on the default landing even at S = 0.3, from
   the sharpened crest's curvature. Single-valuedness came only with the
   classic convergence.
4. **The carve is authored.** `1.03 − 1.4·√(dz_C/h_C)` is a shape chosen to
   open the cavity, not a measured face; it removes crest mass in the ~4 m
   band ahead of the crest phase at plunging stations for the life of the
   ribbon.
5. **Facets** at close range (POV): 24 `u` segments and vertex-FD normals;
   Track D.
6. **The rider** rides the un-carved surface at the lip (surface-query define
   pinned by a test).

`probe.json`. Raw per-vertex readbacks stay in the ignored
`qa/tube-2026-09-24/probe.rows.json`.
