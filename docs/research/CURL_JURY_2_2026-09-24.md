## 1. Verdict

**The site test now runs, and the bore passes it.** At the Second Peak
cliff the five arms are two pictures: `default ≈ tubeclassic ≈ sections`
(1–5 k of 625 k pixels differ) and `bore ≈ all`. The tube and section flags
do not reach the cliff view at these clocks, by design (profile weight ≤ 0.16
at ξ 0.65; no section shuts in the sampled window). Only the bore changes the
site picture, and it changes it the right way: Domain and Field both rank the
bore class first there. Field's ratio table puts `all` at set-wave 54 s at a
total log-distance of **0.15** from the footage (knuckle 1.45 vs 0.8–1.5 face
heights wide, head white 0.58 vs 0.35–0.65 tall, bore band 0.53 vs 0.5–0.65,
top level with the crest); the default family sits at 4.99 with a 3–6 px
feather line for a head. Domain: "the only class that draws a knuckle nose
and a thick round-topped bore level with the crest, peeling the right way, no
cavity."

**The bore's cost is composition, and the Squint seat paid it.** From the
cliff the wedge rides every broken crest in frame, so the inner rows carry a
second and third bright horizontal; Squint counts 4–7 blobs against the
default's 3–5 and calls it "stripes instead of a head", ranking `bore` fourth
overall. Both readings are of the same pixels: the head is right and the
frame has no single subject. The footage has one live head over a quiet grey
sea.

**At Sewers the section arm makes the head.** `sections` and `all` turn the
faceted slab into a compact knuckle on a clean dark dome (Squint first and
second; Domain's Sewers ranking `all` first, `sections` second). The
`sectioncurl` flag takes the steady head to the spilling ξ, so nothing pitches
mid-frame at the old close camera and the head that remains is a knuckle. The
tube ribbon at Sewers 52 s is now a compact hook with an open lens (Track L)
and the jurors still read it as "a sail" / "a fin down the face with a hole
behind it": a cavity on a card day at a spot the bed says spills. Correct per
CURL_TRUTH §3, and the seats say it looks wrong at this site.

**Whitewater material is now the first defect.** Every seat, every arm:
the broken water is a flat white plane with streak sprites at Sewers, and
blurred streaks with leopard patches and specular glitter at the cliff,
where the footage is matte, opaque, lumpy white that ages into a soft foam
field. Field: the bore is right for the first ~1.5 face heights behind the
head and then collapses to a 2–3 px foam line within ~3 face heights; the
footage's bore stays 0.5–0.65 thick for 7.5 s.

**Two things the footage says that no arm does** (Field §"Two things"):
the bore as the wave's second body, continuous for the life of the head; and
**the face is darkest at the top**. Every arm lights the crest and darkens
toward the trough, the inverse of the footage. That is the largest
side-by-side difference at the cliff pose and it is shading, not geometry.

## 3. What every seat agreed on

1. The tube and section flags are invisible from the cliff at these clocks
   and forcings. Correct for the site; nothing to judge there.
2. The bore's head is right and its extent is wrong: too bright too far back
   at the cliff (the inner rows), too short behind the head everywhere.
3. No arm draws a thin, scalloped, short-lived lip line; the knuckle is a
   smooth cap.
4. No section-shutting event (criteria 7–10) appears in the sampled frames.
   Track K's own event is at Second Peak card month, x ≈ 15, 49.2–50.6 s;
   the matrix sampled 52–56 s on the field forcing. Not tested here.
5. Sewers close 56 s is arm-invariant: the head has left the frame. Sample
   the head, not the clock.

## 4. Direction, in order

1. **Whitewater material.** Matte, opaque, lumpy white over a grey base that
   ages into lace; kill the streak sprites and the glitter on broken water.
   This is GRID_FRAG foam material plus the spray sprite look, and it is the
   first thing every juror named.
2. **Bore extent, both ways.** Keep the wedge thick for the head's life
   (footage: 7.5 s, 0.5–0.65 H_f), and restrict it to the live head's line
   from the cliff so the inner rows stop competing. Track I2's stated next
   item (a narrower, brighter knuckle over the first 0.5–1 H_f; bore cap
   0.05–0.1 H_f below the crest) is the same work.
3. **Face shading: dark at the top.** Measure the footage's face luma
   gradient and the render's, then move the face's lighting so the wall top
   is the darkest water in frame. A look change with a registered reference.
4. **The lip line.** A bright scalloped edge a few percent of H_f thick on the
   knuckle, hooks living 0.3–0.7 s. Track H's aerated tip exists on the
   ribbon; the knuckle needs it on the grid.
5. **Test the section event on purpose:** capture K's Second Peak card-month
   shut at its own clocks beside the event-B sheet.
6. **Promotion calls for Andy** (frames in `assets/curl-jury-2-2026-09-24/before-after.jpg`):
   `#gapfix=1` (removes gap heads, no cost found); `#sectioncurl=1` (Sewers
   head becomes a knuckle, invisible at the cliff); `#bore=1` (passes the
   site test, costs composition until item 2). The tube ribbon stays opt-in:
   right shape now, wrong site on any card day.
