# USGS OFR 2007-1270 — every number the public PDF carries, against the model

**Finding.** The report prints no breaking position, no profile and no depth
table; the only quantitative bathymetry it publishes is Fig. 9, a 701 × 527 px
colour-coded SWATHplus map whose graticule and legend let it be georeferenced
and colour-inverted to ±0.3 m (deep) / ±0.6 m (shallow) at 5.3 m/px — and its
swath stops 110–220 m from the coastline at every canon spot, seaward of every
break line the model draws for the report's own wave windows except one. Where
swath and interpolants overlap (110–500 m out), the shipped NCEI 2012 grid is
the closer of the two at four spots and the CUDEM 1/9" at three, with
per-spot mean residuals of −0.8 … +0.4 m against NCEI and −2.3 … +1.5 m against
CUDEM; the one bake that reaches a sounding (The Hook, 1.5 m caption waves)
breaks in 2.8 m of interpolant where the swath reads 5.1 m, an implied
114 m shoreward shift. The timex figures cannot be reduced to a locus: they are
oblique 400 × 300 px frames with no ground control in the water plane, at
ranges where one pixel is 4–66 m of cross-shore distance before any pose error.

## (b) Fig. 9 — the swath map, digitised (p. 9, figure-raster)

**Georeference.** The frame carries five longitude ticks (121°59′30″W …
121°57′30″W at 30″) and three latitude ticks (36°57′30″N … 36°56′30″N at 30″),
read from the 4× upscaled raster. An affine fit through them has 0.2 px
residual and gives **5.29 m/px in x, 5.28 m/px in y** — isotropic, and the
1,000 m scale bar agrees. Overlaying the OSM coastline and the twelve spot
nodes on the orthophoto (`fig9_overlay_osm.jpg`) puts the yellow line on the
cliff base and the apex on the point; the georeference is good to about a
pixel, i.e. ±5 m.

**Colour → depth.** The legend bar (1 m/3 ft at the top, 20 m/66 ft at the
bottom, labels equally spaced) is read row by row into a hue table, and every
saturated map pixel is assigned the depth of the nearest legend hue (hue is
invariant to the hillshade the swath is rendered with). That raw reading is
**not** what the map encodes: against the NCEI grid, which matches the CSMP
2 m soundings to 0.076 m RMS from the 10 m isobath out (`BATHY_SOURCES` §3),
the raw reading runs from −1.0 m at 2–4 m depth to +3.1 m at 16–22 m. The
map's stretch is not the legend's labels. Fitting the legend reading L against
NCEI depth D on the sounding-backed zone (D ≥ 10 m, n = 3,181):

    L = 1.402 D − 2.88        (RMS after 0.28 m; ramp floor 2.8 m, ceiling 16.3 m)

and applying that one affine everywhere leaves the residual flat where it was
not fitted — bin means −0.12 … +0.17 m from 3 m to 10 m, RMS 0.55–0.64 m
(the 0–3 m bin, +0.69 m, is mostly ramp-floor readings). A refit on 3–8 m
alone gives a = 1.21, b = −1.88: the same line within the ramp's noise.
So the digitiser is calibrated on soundings, checked for linearity in the
zone under test, and has a precision of **±0.3 m beyond 10 m and ±0.6 m
inside it**. Readings on the ramp floor are bounds (≤ 2.4–2.8 m), never values;
the report's own statement that the swath reaches "just less 2 m" (p. 10)
says the floor is nearer 2 m than the fit's 2.8, so those bounds are loose.

## (c) The timex figures — why no locus (p. 13)

Figs. 14 and 15 are the 10-minute mean and variance of ~3,000 frames at 5 Hz
from the s5/s18 scenes (the road runs away to the right with the ocean on the
left, which from the printed camera position is the view toward the point,
i.e. "Pleasure Point 1st/3rd peak"; the report does not label the scene). The
camera is printed at **N 36° 57.407′, W 121° 58.254′** = stage (472, 299), on
the bluff 28 m from the coastline between 33rd and 36th Avenues, ground 11.1 m
NAVD88 (CUDEM lidar). No pan/tilt/zoom is printed; the block camera is an 18×
zoom (HFOV 48°–2.8°); the reproduced panels are ~400 × 300 px, JPEG twice.

Range resolution of a water-plane point at range R from a camera h above the
water, per pixel of tilt, is R²/h × (VFOV/rows). With h ≈ 13 m above MSL
(3 m mount assumed):

| spot | range | bearing | depression | m/px at VFOV 36° | m/px at VFOV 10° |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sewer Peak | 403 m | 206° | 1.9° | 25.8 | 7.2 |
| First Peak | 244 m | 182° | 3.1° | 9.5 | 2.6 |
| Second Peak | 153 m | 157° | 4.9° | 3.7 | 1.0 |
| 38th | 289 m | 74° | 2.6° | 13.3 | 3.7 |
| The Hook | 643 m | 64° | 1.2° | 65.8 | 18.3 |

Those are the *floor* on cross-shore precision before pose is solved, and
pose cannot be solved from the PDF: the only water-plane reference in the
frame is the horizon (fixes tilt and roll given the focal length), and pan
and focal length need two surveyed land points in the frame; the OSM
coastline is the bluff base, hidden from a bluff-top camera, and nothing on
the road or railing is surveyed. A breaking band digitised from these panels
would carry a range error of tens of metres at First Peak and over 100 m at
the Hook — larger than the model-vs-plane offsets it would be asked to
discriminate (20–90 m below) — so it is not produced. What the panels do
support, without kinematics (MEASUREMENT_LESSONS 1), is that on 2006-08-15 at
Hs 0.5 m a distinct breaking band existed seaward of the shore in the
variance image, and on 2006-09-20 at 1.5 m it was much wider.

## Model vs report (`scripts/compare_ofr_locus.mjs`)

The bake (`bed.js bakeBreakLine`, the app's default path, read back through
`breakZAt` on the stage grid) was run for every mapped preset at the two timex
windows and the Aug/Sep 2006 monthly means, on the shipped bed in all three
modes (measured + reef, plane, measured) and on the CUDEM-built modules. The
report's Hs is at 13 m; the model's H0 is deep water, so Hs was de-shoaled
with the model's own Ks at 13 m for the window's Tp (Ks 1.03–1.12). The line
is placed on the Fig. 9 profile frame (metres seaward of the OSM coastline
along the spot's shore-normal); the swath's depth there is compared with the
depth the line was baked in, and the difference is turned into a cross-shore
offset through the local NCEI slope (0.007–0.023).

## The honest reading

* The report's bathymetric content, as published, does **not** touch the
  model's break zone. Fig. 9 stops where the model starts; the CPS transects
  that go "into water depths less than 1 m" are drawn as track lines (Fig. 6)
  and never as profiles. The −0.10 m sheet, the reef crest and the bench are
  as unmeasured in public after this exercise as before it.
* What it does give is the first **sounding-backed residual on the two
  interpolants in the 110–500 m band**: the shipped NCEI 2012 grid is right to
  ±0.5 m at five of seven spots there and ~0.8 m too deep at Sewer Peak; the
  CUDEM's coastline-mask flat is 1.1–2.3 m too shallow from 38th to Shark's
  Cove. `BATHY_SOURCES` §5 item 3 (withdrawn in §7) stays withdrawn; the
  CUDEM is the worse *bed* here, not only the worse-provenanced one. Every
  per-spot number in the repo remains "on the NCEI 2012 interpolant", and
  that qualifier now carries a measured error bar for the outer half of the
  reef instead of an inferred one.
* The one model-vs-sounding residual (Hook, 1.5 m day) is against the fitted
  reef, not the plane: the reef window's outer edge on the interpolant is
  1.6–2.0 m shallower than the survey. That is the direction `REEF_FIT_SIGNED`
  would predict if the 2012 grid's smooth ramp is the thing the fit is
  leaning on. It is not enough to re-tune anything (`CUDEM_BED` §10's rule
  stands) but it is enough to say which spot to look at first when the data
  request is answered.
* The wave-climate side is now measured, not hindcast: the AWAC's 211° ± 9°
  and 0.7–0.9 m non-winter Hs are consistent with what SC116 gives the
  presets; the two timex days are on record with their forcing and tide, so
  when the imagery arrives the pre-registration is already written.

## What would falsify

* **The calibration.** If the SWATHplus grid itself (P-1-05-MB) at any
  110–500 m sample differs from the calibrated Fig. 9 reading by more than
  ~1 m, the affine ramp assumption is wrong and the residual tables above are
  void. The check is one grid file.
* **The interpolant verdicts.** A CPS transect (W-1-05-MB) through the Hook or
  Shark's showing the bed at 160–420 m within 0.5 m of the CUDEM's −0.1 …
  −2.4 m would overturn "the CUDEM sheet is too shallow"; one through Sewers
  showing NCEI's −3.3 … −5.7 m at 220–430 m would clear NCEI there.
* **The Hook residual.** If the report's s32 (Hook–38th) timex for
  2006-09-20 11:41, rectified with pose from the report's own bluff lidar,
  shows the mean breaking band centred 150–200 m from the cliff, the model's
  reef line is right and the swath reading at 193 m is wrong (it is 3 samples
  from the swath edge); if the band sits 60–100 m out, the reef fit at the
  Hook is the problem, as read above.
* **The small-wave tension.** The s5/s18 timex for 2006-08-15 12:20, rectified,
  either shows breaking over the First/Third Peak reef at Hs 0.5 m (the
  activation floor is too high, or a monochromatic H0 is the wrong summary of
  a 0.5 m sea) or shows shore break only (the model is right and the
  variance band is inner reform).

None of these needs a new instrument: `compare_ofr_locus.mjs` already takes a
profile JSON and a forcing window, and the CPS/SWATH grids, if they arrive,
drop into `data/bathy/candidates/` on the recipe in `build_candidate_grids.py`.
