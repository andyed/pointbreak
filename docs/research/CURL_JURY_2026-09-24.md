# The curl jury — 2026-09-24

Verdict on the evening's fan-out (TODO.md "THE CURL FAN-OUT"): five arms judged
against the site criteria in [CURL_TRUTH_2026-09-24.md §4](CURL_TRUTH_2026-09-24.md),
by three seated jurors with one lens each. The Stranger seat recused twice
(project context and filenames reached it; see §5). Ballots verbatim in
[assets/curl-jury-2026-09-24/](assets/curl-jury-2026-09-24/).

## 1. Verdict

**No arm passes the site test, and the reason is upstream of the lip.** At the
Second Peak Lookout pose on the field-day forcing (H0 1.4 m, T ~16 s, tide
+0.73 m, the exact conditions of Andy's 2026-08-15 clip) the four simulation
arms are pixel-identical (mean luma difference ≤ 0.01; at most 17 pixels differ
by > 20 levels, all JPEG noise) and **nothing breaks**: three parallel glassy
walls, a 3–5 px white line at the far head where the footage has a 0.35–0.65
face-height bore. Every seat found this independently. It is the peel-floor
tide-band finding of the 2026-09-23 fidelity audit seen from the picture side:
the day sits above the band, the line falls off the reef, and no lip mechanism
can draw a break the model has not declared.

**Among the arms at Sewers, the tube+classic combination has the best shape
and the wrong material.** Two of three seats rank `#tube=1&classic=1&descent=1`
first among simulation arms (Squint: the only head that survives blur as one
compact bright mass over a dark hollow over a darker face; Domain: the only
lip with water-like curvature at a plausible 0.3–0.4 face-height projection
with nothing standing above the crest). The Field seat ranks it third because
what it adds is a glassy ribbon 1.0–1.5 face heights tall that the footage
never shows; its *white* lip, 0.05–0.10 face heights, is the closest thing in
the matrix to the footage's thin luminous line. The Domain seat's first
defect: "dry barrel", clear glass, no white at the tip, no knuckle, no bore.

**The shipped default is last in two seats and first in one.** Squint and
Domain place it last (a horizontal white slab fused into the sky at 52 s,
standing ~0.2–0.3 face heights above the crest, which is on the must-not list;
faceted "paper boat" at 46 s). The Field seat places it first on throw
geometry alone, because a slab that does not throw is less unlike a spilling
head than any arm that does. Both readings are right: the default's fault is
material and thickness, not a throw.

**The game hack wins "reads as a breaking wave" and fails the site.** Domain
ranks it first on both readability questions (compact white head level with
the crest ahead of a dark face at the cliff view; whitewater trailing the
head). It fails criterion 11 and two must-nots outright: a full cavity on a
Pleasure Point lineup, spray rising 1.1–2.6 face heights above the crest
(footage: ≤ 0.15), and a whitewater strip that is a straight geometric ribbon.

## 3. What every seat agreed on

1. **Nothing fires at Second Peak on the field day.** The arms cannot be
   compared at the site because the model does not break there. The
   discriminating ratios (bore thickness, knuckle width, hook cycle) are
   therefore identical across arms and all wrong.
2. **No simulation arm draws whitewater at the head.** No knuckle 0.8–1.5
   face heights wide, no round-topped bore 0.4–0.65 thick behind it, no lace.
   The Domain seat's closing line: build it as a foam problem, not a geometry
   problem.
3. **The tube geometry is the right direction, on the classic grid.** Track
   B's own measurement stands: `tube` alone moves 1.3–5 % of Sewers pixels
   (it punches a hole in the default slab), while the classic profile moves
   15–34 %. The ribbon needs the single-valued grid under it.
4. **The 50 s clock is blind.** Every arm's face goes to a light textured
   midtone at 50 s and figure/ground disappears; arm differences are legible
   only at 52 s. A future jury should sample 52–56 s, not 46–50.
5. **Rendered exposure is ~1.4× the footage** (face luma 90–140 vs < 95,
   foam 205–225 vs > 185) and the near face carries a specular glitter field
   the overcast footage lacks. Not an arm question; a look question.

## 4. Direction, in order

1. **Make the model break at Second Peak on the field day** before touching
   the lip again. This is the fidelity audit's open peel item (tide band too
   tight by ≥ 0.35 m; `PEEL_BAND_FIELD_2026-09-24.md`, `REEF_REFIT_2026-09-24.md`).
   Until then the site test cannot be run on any arm.
2. **Foam on the ribbon, bore behind the head.** Aerated white at the lip tip
   (the footage's thin line), a knuckle at the head, a thick bore behind it.
   Track C's plume plumbing and the roller are the existing pieces; Track C's
   landing-behind-lip finding (the fold remaps the shared landing 7.8–10 m
   behind the drawn lip at Sewers) must be fixed first or the bore stands on
   the crest's back.
3. **Profile: a thinning roof, not a clearing edge.** Track B's diagnosis and
   the Field seat's "glassy ribbon 1.5 face heights" are the same defect from
   two sides: the post-impact clearing to the foot makes a flap. Change
   `shared/breaker-profile-glsl.js`, not the mesh.
4. **Barrel as a section event.** Per CURL_TRUTH §3 the steady head at every
   spot but First Peak on big long-period days should spill; the crash the
   footage records is a section shutting. Author plunging through `sections`,
   not through a spot `xi`.
5. Borrow from the hack only what the Domain and Squint seats credited: the
   dark unbroken face as ground, a compact white head level with the crest,
   whitewater that trails the head. Not the throw, not the plume height.

  ranking). Each wrote its ballot before seeing another's.

  the question and filenames; the reseat used neutral names and a neutral

  and recent commit subjects load into any subagent started from this
  repository. A clean Stranger needs a launch from outside `~/Documents/dev`.
  Recorded as a limit of in-repo juries, not as a vote.
- Ordinal, not metric: pose and lens differ between the Lookout render and
  the footage; Sewers is a different spot on a plunging card; Sewers face
  height is ±25 % because the trough is a shading junction. Nothing here is a
  realism score.
- Andy's eye has not judged these frames. This note is the jury's input to
  that verdict, not a substitute for it ([visual-work-needs-his-eye]).
