# The model on the CUDEM 1/9" bed: what is stable across two grids (2026-09-01)

*Companion to `BATHY_SOURCES_2026-09-01.md` (the grids), `PRIVATES_CONTOUR_2026-09-01.md`
(the contour fit) and `BREAK_FIELD_2026-09-01.md` (the instrument). Nothing here
changes a shipped default: `pp_bathy.json`, `pp_geo_profiles.js`,
`pp_depth_patches.js` and every runtime import are byte-identical to this
morning's. Everything below runs on siblings of those files behind a flag.*

## Why

The shipped bed is the NCEI 1/3 arc-second DEM (~10 m posts). Today's survey
acquired the NOAA CUDEM 1/9 arc-second tile (~3 m, same datum, same frame) and
a wider cut of the same NCEI DEM. At the seven canon spots the CUDEM node
elevations differ from NCEI by −1.33 to +1.18 m and the 2–3 m breaking
contours rotate 6–20° between grids — larger than the 4–8° peel signal the
model exists to draw. Every derived product in this repo (contour fit, depth
patch, reef fit, break-line bake, activation H0, peel floor) is a function of
the bed. So: what does the model do on the finer bed, and which of its claims
survive the change of grid?

## 1. What was built

**Source-selectable builders.** `build_geo_profiles.py` and
`build_depth_patches.py` take `--bathy FILE`. The shipped grid writes the
shipped modules exactly as before (`npm run check:geo` and `check:depth` pass
unchanged); any other grid writes `pp_geo_profiles.<tag>.js` /
`pp_depth_patches.<tag>.js` next to them, never over them. `null` cells are
handled explicitly: a contour scan that lands on a null post, or a depth patch
that samples one, fails that spot closed (`usable: false`, reason recorded in
`generatedFrom.failedClosed`), as does a spot the grid cannot frame. No grid
built today actually carries nulls; the fail-closed path fired for a different
reason (§3). Products built: `ncei13_wide`, `cudem19`, `cudem19-t05`
(CUDEM with `--truncate 0.5`, the rule from PRIVATES_CONTOUR). All are
committed so the tables below reproduce without a rebuild; nothing imports
them by default.

**Headless bed switch.** `scripts/lib/bed-source.mjs`: `--bed=<tag>` (or
`POINTBREAK_BED`) installs a `node:module` resolve hook that rewrites the two
module specifiers to the tagged siblings for every consumer — `bed.js`,
`params.js`, `main.js`, `incident-direction.js` and the instrument itself —
without editing an import. Without the flag no hook is installed. This is the
mechanism the instrument already used for the bare `three` specifier.
`measure_break_activation.mjs` gained `--bed`, `--map-privates` (gives the
`privates` preset `geoSpot: "Private's"` for one process, so a bed on which
its fit is usable can be measured) and `--mode=card` (the per-spot card-state
table, §4). Tagged runs write to `qa/break-field-<tag>/`, never into the
committed `qa/break-field/summary.json`. The bit-identical gate
(MEASUREMENT_LESSONS 4) ran at every rung of every sweep on every bed: max
|Δz| 0 m, 0 gap mismatches, 0 field difference, 0 alpha difference, on all
3 × 521 H0 rungs, 41 T rungs and 41 tide rungs per spot.

## 2. Instrument check: the wide NCEI grid (lesson 4)

The wide grid is the same DEM on the same lattice, so its products should
reproduce the shipped ones. They almost do, and the "almost" is the noise
floor of every comparison below.

- The two JSONs hold **identical post values** over all 282,361 overlapping
  posts (0 differ). Their lattice origins do not coincide: each file rounds
  `x0, y0` to 0.1 m independently, so the wide grid samples every point
  **0.208 m east and 0.044 m north** of where the shipped grid samples it.
- As committed, the wide products differ from the shipped ones by ΔRMS
  ≤ 0.04 m (Private's −0.08), Δtangent ≤ 0.1°, Δreef-elevation ≤ 0.01 m,
  and a contour-curve displacement of 0.03–0.39 m over the window at the six
  usable spots (0.60 m at Private's). The depth patches differ in 87–98 % of
  their uint16 cells, typically by a few quanta (0.9 mm each), with a
  per-patch maximum of 0.27–0.63 m and 1.02 m at one Sewers cell (a 0.21 m
  shift on a steep face); plane residual RMS identical to 0.01 m at all six.
- **Rebuilding from the wide grid with its origin snapped onto the shipped
  lattice gives products identical to the shipped ones in every data field**
  (geo module byte-identical bar provenance; all six uint16 arrays and every
  patch scalar equal). The residual is the registration, and nothing else.
- What 0.21 m of registration does to the *model*: activation H0 moves
  ≤ 0.002 m; card alpha ≤ 0.3° (Sewers 36.3 → 36.5, The Hook 36.8 → 36.5);
  the flip table is identical rung for rung with jump sizes within 0.4 m;
  the peel-return rung moves by at most one 0.005 m rung; reef-fit β is
  identical to 0.1°; the 10 m contour-walk bearing moves 0–0.8° (Sewers
  142.3 → 143.1). The walk estimator is the least stable instrument here.

**Noise floor, then:** 0.1° on a fitted tangent, 0.8° on a walked normal,
0.3° on a card alpha, 0.002 m on an activation H0, one rung on a threshold,
0.4 m on a flip jump. Every CUDEM difference reported below exceeds its floor
by at least an order of magnitude, so the differences are the data.

**The Hook and Shark's Cove cannot be framed on CUDEM.** The frame takes the
bathymetric gradient at the OSM node with a ±5 m central stencil. On CUDEM
the node sits on a surface stored as **exactly −0.10 m NAVD88 across the whole
27 × 27 m window** (81 posts, every one −0.10; Shark's the same). Widening the
stencil does not rescue it: at the Hook |∇z| is 0.0000 at 5 and 10 m, 0.0003 at
15 m, 0.0019 at 30 m (bearing 163°); at Shark's it is zero out to 20 m and
0.0004 at 30 m (bearing 90°, i.e. noise). On NCEI the same nodes have
|∇z| = 0.016–0.017 at every stencil width, bearing 139–140° and 123–125°. A
constant −0.10 m over hundreds of square metres of an intertidal platform is
a fill or interpolation surface, not soundings; the CUDEM's surf-zone source
is undocumented at the tile level (BATHY_SOURCES §1c), so which it is cannot
be told from the file. The builder fails these two closed and says why.

**Sewer Peak misses the usable floor by 0.03 m** (5.03 vs 5.00). That is a
threshold set on the NCEI grid (MEASUREMENT_LESSONS 5: it was never
calibrated on another). With the PRIVATES_CONTOUR departure rule it passes at
1.52 m on a window that ends 80 m up-point instead of 201 m: the CUDEM contour
leaves the frame there. Its tangent also swings from 11.2° to −8.0°, which
happens to land within 1° of the OSM coast tangent (−7.1°) that the NCEI fit
was 18° away from.

**Second Peak's tangent moves 22.7°** (46.6° → 69.3°) on a fit that stays
usable (1.68 m). On NCEI it was within 0.8° of the coastline; on CUDEM the
contour through the node runs 22° off the coast. §4 shows what the model
makes of that.

NCEI wide reproduces the NCEI column within the §2 floor at every cell
(activation ±0.002, α ±0.3°, β ±0.1°, line z ±0.1 m). h_b is 3.90 / 3.25 /
2.80 / 2.10 / 2.70 / 1.95 / 1.40 m on every grid — it is a function of the

Four things to read off.

1. **Second Peak's peel is a left on CUDEM.** The reef fit converges as
   cleanly as on NCEI (β 52.1°, derived 40.9° against 41°, same two sign
   violations) and the drawn line reads **−0.4°** with 44 of ~100 clean
   stations reversed, against 25.8° and 19 on NCEI. The fit metric is the
   legacy unsigned line bearing (`fitMetric: 'legacy-break-line-bearing'`);
   the canonical signed metric says the same line peels the wrong way over
   most of the stage. This is MODEL.md §4.5's reversal, and it is exactly the
   hazard the "canonical refit deferred" note in `reefFitFor` names: on this
   bed the unsigned fit is satisfied by a wave the signed metric rejects. The
   22.7° tangent swing (§3) is the mechanism — the Ψ contour frame the signed
   alpha is measured in turned with the stage, the break line did not.
2. **Sewers' reef activates later, not earlier, on CUDEM** (1.407 vs 1.239 m,
   at a card of 2.2 m) — the node is 1.33 m deeper there. The August p75 of
   0.585 m (BREAK_FIELD §6) is further below activation than before, so "no
   representation on this bed draws a Sewers peel in summer" holds on both
   grids, more strongly on the finer one. Its card alpha rises to 45.9°
   (target 38) on a stage 121 m shorter, with the drawn line 100 m closer to
   the node.
3. **Private's passes the fit and does not produce a peel.** With
   `--truncate 0.5` the contour fits at 4.34 m and the depth patch builds,
   but the shipped line sits **35 m shoreward of the OSM node** at x = 0
   (88 m as a stage median), on the wedge footprint at only 8 % of stations,
   reading −13.9°: a left, in 1.5 m of water, off the reef. The reef fit
   reports 30.0° against 31° but needed all 14 secant evaluations to get
   there (`REEF_FIT_MAX_ITER`), i.e. it stopped at the tolerance edge by
   best-so-far, not by convergence. The 5 m RMS gate is a statement about a
   contour, not about a wave; passing it is necessary and, here, nowhere near
   sufficient.
4. **Jack's is the invariant.** Activation 0.616 m on both grids to three
   decimals; β 41.9 vs 41.5; α 37.1 vs 40.7; on-reef 0.82 vs 0.81; the line
   19 m further out. The activation match is not a coincidence: the wedge
   lifts the bed toward a crest elevation set by the card (`targetEl` from
   h_b), and wherever the natural bed is shallow enough for the lift to reach
   that ceiling the shallowest reef cell has the same depth on any grid.
   Activation is therefore a property of the *synthetic reef* where the bed
   permits, and of the bed only where the wedge is amplitude-limited (Sewers,
   First Peak, Second Peak: Δ 0.17, 0.04, 0.01 m).

The depth at the drawn line (2.77 / 3.28 / 2.11 m…) is the same on both
grids to 0.01 m at every spot, by construction — it is the γh crossing of a
criterion that depends on H0 and Ks, not on where the bed puts it. It is
stable and it is not site character.

## 10. Verdict

**The CUDEM grid should not become the shipped bed.** Not because the 2012
DEM is better — nothing here says which surface is closer to the reef — but
because the model as built is calibrated to the surface it was built on, and
moving it exposes that calibration at three of six mapped spots:

- two spots cannot be framed because the finer grid stores their nodes on a
  constant −0.10 m surface whose provenance is unknown;
- one spot loses its bed on a 5 m threshold it clears by 0.03 m;
- one spot's peel reverses handedness on a reef fit that still reports
  success, because the fit's metric is not the metric the wave is judged by;
- the one newly-passable spot passes a contour gate and draws a left.

Meanwhile the quantities that *are* the same on both grids are mostly the
synthetic reef's: activation where the wedge reaches its ceiling, the flip
rungs it creates, the criterion depth. The bed-dependent quantities the model
presents as site character — the stage tangent, the breaking contour's
orientation, where the line sits relative to the node, Second Peak's and
Sewers' peel angles — move by 2–5× the signal between two surfaces of the
same datum and frame. On the present evidence they are properties of a DEM,
not of Pleasure Point, and MODEL.md's per-spot numbers for them should be read
with that grid named.

**What would have to be true first**, in order:

1. **A sounding under the break.** Two unvalidated surfaces disagreeing by
   ~1 m at the node is a resolution finding, not a truth (BATHY_SOURCES §4).
   USGS OFR 2007-1270 remains the only surveyed source named; alternatively a
   visual ground truth of the break-line position at one spot on one day
   (`VISUAL_GROUND_TRUTH.md`) would arbitrate the 30–100 m line displacements
   in §4 directly.
2. **The −0.10 m flats explained.** Whether the CUDEM's intertidal surface at
   The Hook and Shark's Cove is a fill, an interpolation, or a lidar return
   at a low-tide platform decides whether those spots have a bed on this grid
   at all. If it is a platform, the OSM node is on dry-at-MLLW rock and the
   frame has to be taken at a contour offshore of it (a contour-segment fit,
   as BATHY_SOURCES §5 already wants for `N_ref`), not at the node.
3. **The reef fit on the signed metric** (the deferred slice 4), or at least a
   fit that fails when the signed alpha it produces is the wrong sign. On
   CUDEM the unsigned fit certifies a left at Second Peak.
4. **The contour floor and the stage rule re-derived on the new grid**, not
   carried over: 5 m, 250 m and `--truncate` 0.5 are NCEI-calibrated
   constants and two of the three cut differently on CUDEM.
5. **`PEEL_FLOOR` re-measured** on whatever bed ships (it is already off-basis
   on today's bake, BREAK_FIELD §4; on CUDEM Second Peak's entry has no
   referent).
6. **Someone looks at the render** on the tagged bed. Everything above is
   headless, and this project has passed automated checks three times while
   the drawn artifact was wrong.

What *can* be taken from the finer grid today is negative and useful: the
model's claimed site character is grid-dependent, the wedge's own behaviour is
not, and the honest per-spot statement is "on the NCEI 2012 DEM, …" until 1
and 2 are done.
