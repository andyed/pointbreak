# The knuckle and the bore: whitewater at and behind the head (`#bore=1`) — 2026-09-24

Track I2 of the second curl wave. Status: **flag-gated prototype, default
byte-identical, not promoted.** Base `main` a3de204, merged to a3e8049 (Tracks
H, I1, J) before the final captures. Instruments: `scripts/probe_bore.mjs`,
`scripts/capture_bore_matrix.mjs`, `scripts/measure_bore_frames.py`,
`scripts/build_bore_table.py`, `scripts/build_bore_sheet.py`. Evidence:
`qa/bore-2026-09-24/` (40 frames, two sheets, probe, measurements, parity;
1.9 MB).

## 1. The problem, restated from the truth and the jury

[CURL_TRUTH §1.2–1.3](CURL_TRUTH_2026-09-24.md): behind a compact knuckle
0.8–1.5 face heights wide, whose top is level with the crest, the footage
draws a thick, round-topped bore of saturated white over grey — 0.5–0.65 H_f
on the outer peel (A), 0.4–0.55 through the section collapse (B), 0.35–0.4 on
the thin clean head (C) — level with or slightly below the crest, thinning as
the wave runs down-point, ageing into lumps and lace over seconds. The face
ahead of the knuckle stays dark. [CURL_JURY §3.2](CURL_JURY_2026-09-24.md):
no simulation arm draws any of this; the Field seat read the head as a white
line 0.06–0.12 H_f — three to five times too thin — over glossy dark water
with specular glints.

This is a foam volume problem and a foam coverage problem, and the fix has to
live in the foam path without becoming a second break authority
([NEXT_INVESTMENTS §2](../NEXT_INVESTMENTS.md)).

## 2. Why the shipped bore cannot draw it (GPU probe)

`scripts/probe_bore.mjs` runs the page's own `SURFACE_PRELUDE + SURFACE_GLSL`
as a float pass (the `probe_tube` pattern, no CPU twin) on shore-normal
transects at a sweep of stations, finds the first crest inside the line, its
front-face trough, and reads the model's `foam` down the face. Default arm,
Track J's field-day rig (`preset=secondpeak&cam=cliff&day=overhead&h0=1.40&tide=0.500`,
T 16), sim 52:

| 2.9 | 4.84 | −2.3 | 0.89 | 0.83 |

(Full tables for all three rigs and both clocks in `qa/bore-2026-09-24/probe.json`;
the jury's lookout rig and Sewers read the same way.)

Two facts, both structural, neither a tuning matter:

1. **The shipped bore band is not on the front face.** `boreBand` in `ocean()`
   is a Gaussian a few metres wide centred on `life.y = zb + frontSpeed·age`,
   `frontSpeed = mix(2.4, 4.1, plunge)` = 2.4 m/s on a spilling card. The
   crest it belongs to leaves the line at its own celerity (~4–5 m/s in the
   break depth), so the band falls behind the crest at ~1.7 m/s: −2.3 m at
   2.9 s, −7.7 m at 5.1 s, −13.8 m at 9.1 s — on the back face and in the
   trough. It is the wake, not the wedge. The white run down the front face
   is 0.8–1.0 H_f for the first two seconds (that is the impact band and the
   pocket, still near the crest) and 0.11–0.43 from four seconds on.
2. **It dies at 3.8 s.** `life.w` hard-zeros at `BORE_END_S = 3.80`; the
   footage bore runs eight seconds and more behind the head (event A: the
   band fills the 400 px crop, the head advances ~1 H_f per second).

So the existing structural mound (`0.27·boreBand·u_H0·u_moundH`) is not the
aerated wedge and cannot be re-budgeted into one: its locus and its clock are
both wrong for the job. What was done instead is the next-cleanest thing
under the one-authority rule — **relocate the bore's volume, don't add a
second one**: the wedge takes the bore's mound budget (the `0.27·boreBand`
term is withdrawn at the same gain) and reads every "where" and "when" from
the existing authorities.

The fragment compounds the thinness: everything on the front face has
`tSince ≈ T` on the carrier clock, so `ageK ≈ 1` beyond the 2.4 s wrap ramp
and the shipped material films it (`foamM *= 0.70`, `foamCol → filmCol`,
`freshCore = 0`). Whatever the model paints on the lower face renders as
grey-blue film — the jury's "glossy dark water". Only the top ~5 m reads
fresh.

## 3. What `#bore=1` builds

`shared/model-glsl.js`, everything under `#ifdef BORE` (the `#roller`
precedent: a default boot compiles the pristine text):

**`boreWedgeAt(xz, t, permission, out ageB, out fracB)`** — one function, read
in both stages at the source coordinate.

- *Locus:* the carrier phase. Crests sit at θ = 2πm; the crest just seaward of
  this water is `m = ceil(θ/2π)` and `ph = 2πm − θ` runs down its front face.
  The wedge rides WITH the crest by construction (Longuet-Higgins & Turner's
  wedge on the front face; Duncan's bulge).
- *Clock:* the lifecycle's own age for **that** crest: `ageB = (θ_break −
  2πm)/ω`, where `θ_break` is the phase at the break line — exactly
  `breakerLifecycleAtX`'s `mod(θ_break, 2π)/ω` for the first crest inside the
  line, `+ T` per crest further in, and negative for a crest that has not
  crossed yet. Causal without a wrap gate; no second clock is minted (test
  `the wedge must not mint a second lifecycle clock`).
- *Permission:* the break's own `brkW` (reef window × section mask, unioned
  with depth's gate) — `ocean()` passes its own, the fragment recomputes the
  identical expression (`breakPermissionAt`). A section gap or an inactive
  reef gets no wedge.
- *Depth on the face:* `fracB` is the **vertical** drop from the crest as a
  fraction of the carrier's range, read off the carrier's own
  `crestShape(−θ, q)` with `ocean()`'s q schedule and pitch skew. The first
  cut used the cosine drop `(1 − cos ph)/2`; with q = 2–4 the peaked profile
  has fallen 75–95 % of its range by 96° of phase and the wedge covered the
  whole face (probe v1, `w30 = 1.0` everywhere). The shape's own drop puts
  0.62 where 0.62 of the face is.
- *Gain:* birth ramp `smoothstep(0.18, BORE_BIRTH_S 0.55, ageB)` (the bulge
  forms as the impact releases; `BORE_BIRTH_S ≥ CRASH_PEAK_S` is a test) ×
  line attachment × permission × set envelope² × `exp(−ageB/BORE_TAU_S 30)`.
  The slow fade is not the thinning — the footage ratio holds 0.5–0.75 to
  7.5 s while the face shrinks — it only subordinates inner crests (age + T
  → ×0.55, criterion 6). The thinning comes from the carrier amplitude, which
  already carries `postBreakHeightRetention`.

**Geometry** (`ocean()`, after the structural mound): `h += BORE_H_FRAC 0.18 ·
(2·amp) · boreBulge(fracB) · gain · lumps`, physical metres, VIS at the end.
`boreBulge = sin(π·fracB/D)` on the front face: **exactly zero at the crest**
(the top stays level with it — nothing in the footage rises above the crest
line), zero at the wedge foot `D = BORE_DEPTH_FRAC 0.62`, round between, and
the test pins `BORE_H_FRAC < D/2` so the bulge's peak cannot clear the crest.
The lump lattice lives in the crest's frame `(x, fracB)` and rolls over on
the seconds clock. `h −= u_bore · 0.27·boreBand·…` withdraws the shipped bore
mound; the impact mound (`0.62·impactBand`) is a different event and stays.
Measured wedge height (probe, `u_bore` 1 → 0 differenced): 0.31–0.72 m
displayed on a 2.6–5.6 m face at the field day, 0.57–1.0 m on 2–9 m at Sewers.

**Foam** (`ocean()`): `foam = max(foam, gain · boreShape(fracB) · foamSizeAt)`
— a floor like the pocket's, so the erosion downstream carves lace into it
but cannot carve it away. `boreShape` is full over the upper D of the face
with a thin cap onto the back so the top reads round, not cut.

**Material** (`GRID_FRAG`, `#ifdef BORE`): the same function at `sourceXZ`;
white lumps over a grey base in the crest's frame (~3/4 of the lattice
white; the hollows a light grey that darkens as the lace opens); the knuckle
— the freshest `BORE_KNUCKLE_S 1.2` s — pushed toward solid white; ageing
into lace on the **crest's** clock `ageB` (2 → 9 s) through the existing `er`
erosion lattice, not on `tSince`; shading placed after the film mix like the
roller's, and the final `col` mix re-applied at the wedge's own weight so
the carrier-clock `ageK` dimming cannot thin it. `specKill` already kills the
glints where `foamM` is floored.

**Plumbing** (`main.js`): `BORE_BUILD` from `#bore=` (gain in [0, 3]), the
`BORE` define on the grid, spray, curtain and tube materials and on the
`curlProbe` instrument, `__pointbreak.setBore(g)` / `bore()` / `boreBuild`.
The rider's `surfaceQuery` keeps its pinned defines literal (the `#tube`
precedent), so under the flag the rider rides the un-wedged grid — a limit,
§6. `docs/CONTROLS.md` row `bore`. Tests: two new cases in
`tests/breaker-anatomy.test.js` (structure pins: off by default, build-gated,
shared authorities, zero at the crest, mound withdrawn; constant relations).

## 4. Field vs render: the white band's share of the face

Method: the Field seat's luma runs (`assets/curl-truth-2026-09-24/measurements.json`),
band `bright_h` over the unbroken face `face_h` read at the same frame. Render:
`scripts/measure_bore_frames.py`, **head-relative** on the cliff rigs — the
head is the column where the band begins along the crest, H_f is the median
dark (teal, luma < 160) run over the 15–60 columns of unbroken face ahead of
it, the band (neutral, luma > 185, the footage's own threshold) is read at
fixed along-crest distances behind it. A right-hander seen from shore peels to
the viewer's left, so the face is left of the head and the bore right of it,
the field sheets' layout. Windows are pinned per rig (rows 284–336, the far

along the near crest reads 77–94 px thick against the default's glitter
(`sheet` not built for it). **Sewers close** is a plunging card where the
shipped impact and splash foam already saturate the head: the probe reads

two arms measure alike from 54 s (0.44/0.55 vs 0.47/0.55); the wedge's
contribution there is the material and the withdrawn mound, and the impact

## 6. Limits and what to fix next

- **The knuckle is not yet a distinct thing.** The band begins at the head at
  full thickness (0.46–0.50 at 0–15 columns vs 0.44–0.50 behind); the footage
  knuckle is a compact lump, brighter than the bore behind it, with the bore
  *slightly below* the crest behind it (B: 0.2 H_f below the wall). The
  `boreK` boost whitens the freshest 1.2 s but nothing measured it, and the
  bore top runs level with the crest all the way back. **Fix next:** give the
  knuckle its own compactness — a stronger, narrower bulge for the first
  ~0.5–1.0 H_f of along-crest travel behind the birth (the `hump` locus
  without the hump's height), and let the bore's cap sit 0.05–0.1 H_f below
  the crest behind it so the knuckle reads as the high point.
- The wedge is defined on the carrier; at a plunging station the drawn face
  includes the bend, the impact mound and the lip facets, so 0.62 of the
  carrier's drop is a smaller share of the drawn face (Sewers, §4).
- The rider's `surfaceQuery` does not carry the define (pinned literal), so a
  `#bore` boot displaces the rider on the un-wedged grid; the wedge is young
  where the rider sits, so the offset is at most the birth ramp's fraction of
  0.18·range.
- `breakPermissionAt` duplicates `ocean()`'s `brkW` expression for the
  fragment (six model calls per fragment under the flag); a shared helper
  would remove the duplication but touches shipped text.
- The head-relative detector is automatic and coarse: it found the live head
  at 50–54 s on the field day and at 50–52 s on the card-day cliff, and a
  small-face stretch of the same wave at the other clocks (starred rows).
  Hand-placed boxes, as the Field seat used, would be the next step for a
  jury; the sheets are built for that.
- No frame here has been judged by Andy's eye ([visual-work-needs-his-eye]).
