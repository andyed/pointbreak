# Squint seat — ballot

Lens: 1/8 downscale, Gaussian r=2, back up to 400 px. Judged only from the companions in this
directory (`*_squint.jpg`) via the contact sheets `sheet_secondpeak_lookout.jpg`,
`sheet_sewers_close.jpg`, `sheet_hack.jpg`, plus 2x magnified water-band crops
(`sheet_*_mag.jpg`) and a diff map of each arm against default (`sheet_sewers_close_diff.jpg`).
No physics, texture, or colour opinions.

## Two facts that frame everything below

1. **secondpeak_lookout does not discriminate arms.** At squint scale all four arms are
   pixel-identical at every clock (max grey difference 2/255 across every pair = JPEG noise).
   Anything said about arms comes from `sewers_close`; secondpeak is judged once as a rig.
2. **sewers_close has two families, not four arms.** default ≈ tube (mean diff 0.4–0.7 at
   t=46–50, only a head-sized patch at t=52) and classicdescent ≈ tubeclassic (mean diff
   0.4–0.65). The descent family differs from the default family across the whole face
   (mean 3.5 → 8.6 rising with clock), not just at the head. Tube vs non-tube is a single
   blob at the right-hand head, visible only at t=52.

## (d) Ranked — which head survives the squint

1. **tubeclassic** — sewers_close_tubeclassic_52: the only frame in the matrix where the head is a
   compact bright lump with a dark hollow under it and a darker face under that. One subject.
2. **hack (pocket)** — pocket_22p6_pitch: the head is a genuine compact knuckle on the cleanest dark
   face of the set, but the frame's brightest mass is the vertical column at bottom-centre, so the
   head is not what the eye lands on. Fix the column and this rig wins outright.
3. **classicdescent** — sewers_close_classicdescent_52: bright mass is gathered and sits on a darker
   face, but without the hollow it reads as a lump on a wall rather than a knuckle; at t=46/48 the
   lower-left foam dash still splits attention.
4. **tube** — sewers_close_tube_52: the hollow is there but the lip above it is still default's flat
   sky-bright bar, so it reads as a notched band, not a knot.
5. **default** — sewers_close_default_52: head is a full-width horizontal white slab fused into the
   sky; t=46 has three equal patches. Nothing at the head survives the blur as a shape.

Below the ranking, not an arm: **secondpeak_lookout** — the head is detached polygons on a midtone
field at every clock and no arm changes a pixel of it at squint scale. If the arms are meant to be
visible from that camera, they are not.

## One cross-arm note (picture-only)

t=50 is the weak clock for every arm on sewers_close: the whole face goes to a light textured
midtone and figure/ground disappears regardless of arm. The arm differences only become legible at
t=52. A jury comparing arms on t=48 or t=50 alone would see nothing.
