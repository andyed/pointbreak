# Private's contour fit: why it fails, what moves it (2026-09-01)

`build_geo_profiles.py` fits `z = c2·x² + c3·x³` to the NCEI equal-elevation
contour through each canon surf node, in a local stage frame. Six of seven
spots fit at 0.22–1.53 m RMS. Private's fits at **16.62 m** (44 samples), fails
the 5 m usable floor, and falls closed to the synthetic stage with no bed, which
disables depth-limited breaking there.

This note tests the three inflators a code reading proposed, runs a 30-variant
sweep over reference elevation × stage window × branch selection at all seven
spots, and records the one rule that brings Private's under the floor without
touching the six. Nothing ships by default. The rule is behind
`build_geo_profiles.py --truncate SLOPE`.

Everything here is generated by `data/model/experiments/contour_variants.py`
(imports the build script, writes nothing). Full per-variant tables, all seven
spots each: [`assets/privates_contour_sweep_2026-09-01.md`](assets/privates_contour_sweep_2026-09-01.md).

## Result in one paragraph

The Private's contour is not ragged and the branch selection never fires. The
selected −0.52 m contour is as smooth as the six (0.68 m RMS about its own
5-point moving average, versus 0.15–0.89 m for the six), it has zero branch
jumps, and the continuity-tracking selector picks the identical point on every
one of the 44 lines. What it does is **turn**: from x ≈ 70 m down-point of the
OSM node it swings 33–38° shoreward for 90 m and recedes to an 85 m plateau by
x ≈ 240 m — the rock platform ending at the cove toward Trees. An
origin-constrained cubic cannot hold a flat approach on one side and a plateau
on the other, so its RMS grows monotonically with the window: 0.46 m at ±50,
1.39 at ±100, 4.57 at ±150, 15.57 at ±250. Ending the stage where the contour
departs the frame (adjacent-line step > 0.5 × 10 m, i.e. > 27° off the tangent)
gives Private's a window of [−189.7, 60] m, **1.87 m RMS on 25 samples**, and
leaves all six mapped profiles byte-identical. The rule passes for every slope
threshold from 0.4 to 0.7; below 0.4 it clips Sewer Peak and The Hook, at 0.8
and above it does nothing.

## Setup

**Factors** (all combinations run at all seven spots; gates evaluated per run):

| factor | levels |
|---|---|
| A. reference elevation | node elevation (baseline); node −0.5 m; node −1.0 m; fixed −2.0 m NAVD88; fixed −3.0 m NAVD88 |
| B. stage window | neighbour midpoints capped at 250 m (baseline); symmetric ±min(150, half the nearer gap); fixed ±100 |
| C. branch selection | nearest to the origin line, `min(candidates, key=abs)` (baseline); continuity tracking outward from x = 0, seeded at 0 |
| D. fit form (reported, not candidates) | `x + x² + x³` through the origin; free cubic `c0..c3`; RMS about a 5-point moving average of the data ("MA5") |

For a non-node reference elevation the constrained fit still has to pass
through (0, 0), so the frame origin slides shore-normal from the node to the
nearest crossing of the target elevation and the tangent is re-taken there
(`_frame_at`). The shore-normal offset and the new tangent are reported; a fit
that moves the frame is a different site, whatever its RMS.

**Gates.** A variant passes only if (i) Private's RMS ≤ 5 m and (ii) for each
of the six mapped spots, the largest |Δz| between the variant's runtime curve
and the baseline's over the baseline window — the `bed.js spotContourCurve`
twin, clamped to each fit's own `stageBoundsM` — is ≤ that spot's own baseline
RMS. The clamp matters: a window change moves the six's bounds, which the
runtime reads for both the contour clamp and the ride span, so it is a
behaviour change even when the coefficients barely move. The asset tables carry
a coefficients-only |Δz| column alongside.

**Data.** NCEI 1/3 arc-second, texel 8.228 × 10.296 m, NAVD88. Scan lines every
10 m along the stage, ±150 m shore-normal at 1 m. Branch-jump threshold for the
diagnostics: 15 m between adjacent lines.

## The three claimed inflators

### (1) Reference elevation — refuted as the cause

Claim: the node sits at −0.52 m NAVD88, so its contour is the ragged edge of
the intertidal platform; a deeper contour would be smoother.

| ref | Private's target NAVD88 | ref offset m | tangent ° | n | RMS x²+x³ | RMS MA5 | lines multi / empty | jumps | six median RMS | six max RMS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| node | -0.52 | 0.0 | 38.9 | 44 | 16.62 | 0.68 | 1 / 0 | 0 | 1.16 | 1.53 |
| node-0.5 | -1.02 | -56.1 | 47.2 | 44 | 9.50 | 0.73 | 0 / 0 | 0 | 1.09 | 2.22 |
| node-1.0 | -1.52 | -92.4 | 60.5 | 44 | 9.39 | 1.08 | 0 / 0 | 0 | 1.81 | 2.80 |
| fix-2.0 | -2.00 | -116.4 | 56.2 | 44 | 5.42 | 0.76 | 0 / 0 | 0 | 1.11 | 3.20 |
| fix-3.0 | not crossed within ±150 m of the origin line | | | | | | | | 2.94 | 9.04 |

(Window and branch at baseline.) The roughness never moves: MA5 is 0.68–1.08 m
at every reference, so the −0.52 m contour was never ragged. RMS does fall with
depth (16.6 → 9.5 → 9.4 → 5.4) because a deeper contour turns less sharply into
the cove, but none reaches the floor at the baseline window, and each one
relocates the site: the frame origin moves 56–116 m seaward and the stage
tangent swings +8° to +22°. The fixed −3.0 m contour does not exist within 150 m
of the node's scan line (shore slope 0.011), and at the six it lifts the worst
fit to 9.04 m. Note the bathymetry file itself records the NAVD88→MSL conversion
as unresolved, so "the node is intertidal" is not a number this repo has
established either way; the fit is datum-independent and this test did not need
it.

over a coast whose OSM tangent swings 56° → 99°.

The mechanism is more specific than "the coast tangent swings". Adjacent-line
steps of the selected contour, walking down-point from x = 0 (m shore-normal
per 10 m along): 0.6, 1.1, 0.2, 1.6, 2.3, 4.0, **6.6, 6.6, 7.8, 7.2, 7.1, 6.5,

largest step is 2.4. The −0.52 m contour holds the frame to x ≈ 60, then turns
33–38° shoreward for ten consecutive lines, then straightens on a new line 85 m
inshore. That is a platform edge receding into a bay, and the OSM-midpoint
window, which the README already says is not a measured reef edge, runs 190 m
past it.

**0 of 30 pass.** Nine variants get Private's under 5 m (every ±100 window,
every ±150 window except node −1.0, and fixed −2.0 at the neighbour window at
5.42 — just over), and every one of them does it by shrinking the window for
all seven spots, which moves the six's `stageBoundsM` and refits them on fewer
points, failing the second gate by 9–45× their own RMS. It is not only the
clamp: at sym ≤ 150 the coefficients-only |Δz| at the six is 1.5–14.2 m
(Second Peak 14.2, Shark's Cove 7.5, The Hook 5.7) against baseline RMS of
0.22–1.53. Reference elevation alone never gets there; branch selection never
changes a number.

## The rule that passes: end the stage where the contour departs the frame

Walking outward from x = 0 on each side, the stage ends at the last line before
the selected contour steps more than `slope × 10 m` shore-normal between
adjacent lines, i.e. before it has turned more than `atan(slope)` from the stage
tangent. A side that never departs keeps its neighbour-midpoint bound. Global
rule, applied to all seven; the six's largest steps are 0.4–3.7 m.

The passing band is slope ∈ [0.4, 0.7]. Its lower edge is Sewer Peak's single
3.7 m step (0.37), texel noise on a platform that does not turn (its MA5 is
0.89 m and it has no run of large steps); its upper edge is the first step of
Private's own turn (7.8 m, 0.78). Inside the band the rule is not on a
knife-edge in the sense of lesson 14 — moving the threshold moves Private's
stage end by one or two lines (50 → 60 → 80 m) and changes nothing else — but
the band is a factor of two wide, and it is bounded on one side by one noisy
step at one spot. A sustained form (two consecutive over-threshold steps) would
widen the lower edge; it was not built and is not measured here.

**Chosen point: slope 0.5** (27°), the centre of the band. `build_geo_profiles.py
--truncate 0.5` produces a module identical to the shipped one at all six
mapped spots except for a `contourFit.variant` provenance block (which the
runtime ignores), and for Private's:

## Recommendation

Do not flip the default in this change. The flag exists and the numbers are
recorded; enabling Private's is a separate, visible decision because it needs
three more edits and one look:

1. `npm run build:geo -- --truncate 0.5` (regenerates `pp_geo_profiles.js`;
   `check:geo` then has to be run with the same flag, or the flag has to
   become the default in `DEFAULT_OPTIONS`).
2. `shared/params.js`: the `privates` preset ships `geoSpot: null`; it would
   need `"Private's"`.
3. `tests/geo-model.test.js` asserts `privates: null` and that the synthetic
   quadratic is what Private's draws; both expectations flip.
4. Someone looks at the render. Automated checks have passed three times on
   this project while the drawn artifact was wrong. What the rule asserts,
   physically, is that Private's reef window ends 60 m down-point of the OSM
   node and the platform edge recedes into the cove toward Trees beyond that.
   Whether that is where Private's actually breaks is a ground-truth question
   (`VISUAL_GROUND_TRUTH.md`, `PP_VISITORS_GUIDE_NOTES.md`) the DEM cannot
   settle, and a 250 m ride span becoming a 60 m down-point span will be
   visible.

If it is enabled, the shipped constant should be the band, not the point:
record 0.4–0.7 next to 0.5 so the next person knows how far it can move
before Sewer Peak is clipped.

## Bounds of every sweep (state the range with the null)

- Reference elevation: node, node −0.5, node −1.0, fixed −2.0, fixed −3.0 m
  NAVD88. Not swept: anything deeper than −3.0 (unreachable within the ±150 m
  scan at Private's anyway), anything shallower than the node, non-integer
  fixed levels between −2 and −3.
- Window: symmetric ±50 to ±250 in 25 m steps; neighbour-midpoint cap 100 to
  250 in 10 m steps; departure slope 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.35, 0.4, 0.45, 0.5, 0.6,
  0.7, 0.8, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0. Not swept: asymmetric fixed windows, caps below 100,
  slopes between 0.7 and 0.8 (the band's upper edge is therefore known to lie
  in (0.7, 0.78]).
- Branch: nearest-to-origin and continuity-tracking only. No Viterbi; lesson 14
  already found that worse than greedy on a related lattice, and here the
  candidate set is single-valued so no selector can differ.
- Fit forms: `x² + x³` (constrained), `x + x² + x³` (constrained, free linear),
  free cubic, MA5. Not tried as runtime forms: anything with a sigmoid or
  piecewise shape, which is what the Private's data would actually want if the
  window were kept.
- Everything at 10 m line spacing, ±150 m scan, 1 m shore-normal sampling,
  one DEM. The 15 m jump threshold only labels diagnostics; no result depends
  on it (the max step anywhere is 7.8 m).
